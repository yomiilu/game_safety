# Never Be Sure You're Safe

A hand-drawn, scroll-driven story game about staying safe while travelling alone.

You follow a young traveller on his first solo trip. You move the story forward by scrolling. At key moments you make the choice for him, and every choice costs or saves two things: **money** and **safety**. Both are shown in the header and change as the story goes on.

## Story

- **Chapter 1: Arrival.** At the airport the official taxi line is an hour long, and a friendly stranger offers a ride right away, and cheap. Do you take it?
- **Chapter 2: Hotel and dinner.** After check-in you choose dinner: order delivery to your room, or go to the hotel café. Each option has its own surprises.
- **Chapter 3: A walk in the city.** You pick a route: a quiet shortcut through a dark alley, or a busy street. In the alley you must remember the turns and run back the same way before you're caught. On the crowded street you must spot the pickpocket while someone distracts you.
- **Finale.** You end the trip with whatever money and safety you have left, and your phone lights up with messages.

After each chapter a notebook opens with short, practical travel safety tips about what just happened, plus a few doodles in the margins.

## Features

- Black and white hand-drawn art with torn-paper outlines
- Scroll-driven scenes, dialogue typed out letter by letter, and a moving "camera"
- Branching choices that change the outcome and the numbers
- Mini-games: the escape run through the alley and spotting the thief in the crowd
- Sound: typewriter, heartbeat, doors, pencil on paper, page flips and notifications, with a volume control
- Works on desktop and mobile
